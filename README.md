## 📋 About

**Taskela AI** is a modern, AI-powered workplace productivity suite designed to automate the repetitive tasks that consume professionals' time — drafting emails, summarizing meetings, planning schedules, and conducting research.

Professionals across industries spend significant time on repetitive tasks. Taskela AI solves this by providing **5 intelligent AI tools** in one elegant dashboard, so you can focus on the work that truly matters.

> 🔬 *Demo Mode — Responses are simulated to showcase the app's architecture and prompt engineering. In production, these connect to AI models like GPT-4 or Claude.*

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

Taskela AI's design sits at the intersection of three frameworks:

| Framework | Influence |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Wassily Kandinsky's Synesthetic Color Theory** | Each color in the app maps to a specific sound and emotion from Kandinsky's 1911 treatise *"Concerning the Spiritual in Art"*. He had synesthesia — he could literally hear colors and see music. |
| **Vogue Editorial Typography** | The brand identity uses Didone-inspired typography principles — extreme contrast, generous negative space, and the confidence of restraint. |
| **McKinsey's Business Value of Design** | McKinsey's research found that top-quartile design companies see **32% higher revenue growth** and **56% higher shareholder returns**. Every design choice in Taskela AI is intentional and measurable. |

## 🛠️ Features

### 1. 🤖 AI Chatbot Interface
> *Kandinsky Color: Deep Blue (#1A3A6B) — The sound of a cello*

A conversational AI assistant for workplace queries. Ask anything — plan your day, draft notes, or get quick answers. Includes suggested starter prompts and a clean chat interface with typing indicators.

### 2. ✉️ Smart Email Generator
> *Kandinsky Color: Warm Gold (#F5B731) — The sound of a trumpet*

Generate professional emails tailored to your audience. Select the recipient role (Client, Manager, Team), choose a tone (Formal, Friendly, Persuasive), describe the context, and receive a fully formatted email with subject line, greeting, body, and sign-off.

### 3. ✅ AI Task Planner
> *Kandinsky Color: Vermilion Red (#E63946) — The sound of a tuba*

Transform goals into structured, prioritized daily or weekly plans. Each task includes time blocks, priority badges (Urgent/High/Medium/Low), and AI-generated time optimization suggestions.

### 4. 📋 Meeting Notes Summarizer
> *Kandinsky Color: Warm Orange (#F4845F) — The sound of a church bell*

Paste meeting notes or transcripts and receive structured summaries with Key Decisions, Action Items (with owners and deadlines), and Important Deadlines — all in clear, scannable cards.

### 5. 🔍 AI Research Assistant
> *Kandinsky Color: Teal Green (#2A9D8F) — The sound of a quiet violin*

Get structured research on any topic with an Overview, Key Insights, Recommendations, and a Simplified Explanation that makes complex information accessible.

## 🧠 Prompt Engineering (Transparency by Design)

Every AI tool in Taskela AI includes a **"View Prompt"** section that reveals the exact prompt used to generate the output. This is a deliberate design choice for transparency and education:

- **Demonstrates prompt engineering skill** — each prompt is crafted with role, context, tone, and format instructions
- **Supports Responsible AI** — users can see exactly how outputs are generated
- **Enables learning** — users can copy, modify, and improve the prompts for their own use

Example prompt structure:
```
Generate a {tone} email to a {role} about: {topic}.
Tone: {tone}.
Ensure professional language, clear structure, and a courteous closing.
Include a subject line, greeting, 2-3 body paragraphs, and sign-off.
```

## 🛡️ Responsible AI

Taskela AI is built with responsible AI principles at its core:

| Principle | Implementation |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Transparency** | Every tool shows the exact prompt used via "View Prompt" sections |
| **Limitations** | Clear disclaimer: "AI can make mistakes. Always review and verify." |
| **Data Privacy** | No sensitive data storage — all processing is client-side |
| **Human Oversight** | "AI assists, humans decide" — tools support, not replace, judgment |
| **Bias Awareness** | Users are encouraged to critically review all AI outputs |
| **Demo Disclaimer** | Per-tool banner: "🔬 Demo Mode — Responses are simulated" |
